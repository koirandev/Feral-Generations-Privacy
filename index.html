Privacy Policy for Feral Generations Effective Date: October 31, 2026 Last updated: October 31, 2026 This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you play Feral Generations (the "Game") on Android, and how it is used. The Game is made by Koiran ("I", "me"), an independent developer based in Finland. If you have any questions, contact me at koirandev@gmail.com 1. The short version - This game has no accounts and I do not collect your name, email, or other personal details. - Your game progress is only saved on your device. - The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, and in the EU/EEA and UK it asks for your consent first. - Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details. 2. Information stored on your device The Game saves your progress (your current animal and bloodline, unlocked animals, best scores, settings and which purchases you own) in files on your device. This data is not sent to me or to any server I run. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes it. The data cannot be recovered after lost. 3. Advertising (Google AdMob) The Game uses Google AdMob to show ads, for example an optional ad you can watch to revive your animal, and occasional ads after a game over. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as: - your device's advertising ID, - your IP address and approximate location derived from it, - device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version), - information about ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped). Consent in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland: before personalised ads are shown, the Game displays Google's consent form so you can choose whether Google may use your data for personalised advertising. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data, for example for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. You can change your choice at any time in the Game's settings [OR: by reinstalling the Game / via the privacy option in the menu]. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policies: - How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy If you buy "Remove ads", the Game stops showing forced ads. Optional reward ads are then skipped as well. 5. What I do not do - I do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personal information. - I do not sell your data. - The game has no chat, social features or user accounts - The game does not use analytics services of its own. 6. Legal basis (EU/EEA users) Where the GDPR applies: - Personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. - Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention are based on legitimate interests in funding and protecting a free game. - Processing purchases is necessary to perform the contract of the purchase. 7. Your rights If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because I do not hold personal data about you on my own servers, most requests are best handled directly through Google (for advertising and purchase data) at https://myaccount.google.com/. You can still contact me at koirandev@gmail.com and I will help where I can. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (https://tietosuoja.fi/en). 8. Children The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact me and I will help have it removed. 9. Changes to this policy I may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example if the Game adds new features. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. 10. Contact Koiran, email: koirandev@gmail.com
